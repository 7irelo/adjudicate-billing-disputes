# Fynbos Voice Business Master Services Agreement

Fynbos Voice is a fictional business VoIP provider created for a software demonstration. This agreement does not describe any real business. Reference marker: FYNBOS-CANARY-5518.

## 1. Definitions [definitions]

1.1 "Customer" means a juristic person that takes hosted voice or PBX services from Fynbos Voice. {definition}

1.2 "Claim period" means the 60 calendar days after the date an invoice is issued. {definition}

1.3 "Effective termination date" means the 30th calendar day after Fynbos Voice receives a written termination notice. {definition}

## 2. Billing and claims [billing]

2.1 Fynbos Voice issues one invoice for each calendar month. All charges include VAT. {other}

2.2 A billing claim must be made within the claim period. Fynbos Voice gives no credit for a claim made after the claim period, but a customer may ask for a manual review. {limit}

2.3 Where one charge for a service period appears more than once, Fynbos Voice will credit each duplicate in full. {grant}

2.4 Where a call was billed above the contracted rate in force when it was made, Fynbos Voice will credit the difference. {grant}

## 3. Out-of-bundle usage [out_of_bundle]

3.1 All usage beyond an included minute allowance is billable at the contracted rate. No opt-in is required, and no credit is given because an allowance ran out. {limit}

## 4. Spend protection [spend_protection]

4.1 The customer is responsible for setting its own spend limits on its PBX. Fynbos Voice gives no credit because usage was unusually high. {limit}

## 5. Termination [cancellation]

5.1 A customer may terminate by written notice taking effect on the effective termination date. {condition}

5.2 Charges for a service period up to the effective termination date remain payable. {limit}

5.3 Where a recurring charge covers days after the effective termination date, Fynbos Voice will credit those days, prorated by the actual number of calendar days in the month. {grant}

## 6. Roaming [roaming]

6.1 Fynbos Voice does not provide a roaming service. A roaming charge on a Fynbos Voice invoice is billed in error and will be reversed in full. {grant}

## 7. International calls [international]

7.1 International calls are enabled on all PBX trunks and are billable at the contracted rate in force when the call was made. {limit}

## 8. Compromise of customer equipment [fraud]

8.1 The customer is responsible for the security of its PBX, credentials and extensions. Charges for calls that result from a compromise of customer equipment remain payable by the customer. {limit}

8.2 Fynbos Voice refers every suspected compromise to its fraud desk. No credit or final liability decision is made until the fraud desk has reviewed the records. {procedure}

## 9. Credits and limits [credits]

9.1 A credit never exceeds the amount charged less any credit already issued against the same charge. {limit}

9.2 Credits of R2,500.00 or less may be approved automatically. A larger credit needs approval from a commercial manager. {procedure}

9.3 A customer that is unhappy with the outcome of a claim may escalate it under the dispute resolution procedure in its order form. {procedure}
