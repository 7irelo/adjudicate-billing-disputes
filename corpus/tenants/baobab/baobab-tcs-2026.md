# Baobab Mobile Consumer Terms and Conditions

Baobab Mobile is a fictional operator created for a software demonstration. These terms do not describe any real network. Reference marker: BAOBAB-CANARY-7741.

## 1. Definitions [definitions]

1.1 "Billing cycle" means one calendar month, from the first day to the last day of the month, in South African Standard Time. {definition}

1.2 "Query window" means the 60 calendar days after the date an invoice is issued. {definition}

1.3 "Effective cancellation date" means the last day of the notice period given under clause 5.1. {definition}

## 2. Billing and queries [billing]

2.1 Baobab Mobile issues one invoice for each billing cycle. All charges include VAT. {other}

2.2 A customer may query any charge within the query window. A query raised after the query window will not result in a credit under these terms, but the customer may still ask for a manual review. {limit}

2.3 Where the same charge for the same service and the same service period appears more than once, Baobab Mobile will credit every duplicate in full and keep one charge. {grant}

2.4 Where a usage charge was rated above the tariff in force when the call was made, Baobab Mobile will credit the difference between the amount billed and the correct amount. {grant}

## 3. Out-of-bundle usage [out_of_bundle]

3.1 Once a bundle is depleted, out-of-bundle usage may be charged only if the customer has opted in to out-of-bundle billing and the opt-in was active for the whole service period of the charge. {condition}

3.2 Where out-of-bundle usage was charged with no active opt-in, Baobab Mobile will reverse the out-of-bundle charge in full. {grant}

3.3 Baobab Mobile sends a bundle depletion notification by SMS when a bundle runs out. {procedure}

## 4. Spend protection [spend_protection]

4.1 The bill-shock threshold is R750.00 of usage charges in one billing cycle. {definition}

4.2 Baobab Mobile sends a bill-shock SMS when usage charges reach the bill-shock threshold in a billing cycle. {procedure}

4.3 Where no bill-shock SMS was sent in a billing cycle, Baobab Mobile will credit the usage charges above the bill-shock threshold for that cycle. {grant}

## 5. Cancellation [cancellation]

5.1 A customer may cancel by giving 20 business days' notice. The notice period starts on the business day after Baobab Mobile receives the request. {condition}

5.2 Charges for a service period that falls within the notice period remain payable. {limit}

5.3 Where a recurring charge covers days after the effective cancellation date, Baobab Mobile will credit those days, prorated by the actual number of calendar days in the month. {grant}

## 6. Roaming [roaming]

6.1 Roaming is available only after the customer activates it. Roaming charges incurred with an active roaming activation are payable. {limit}

6.2 Where roaming usage was charged with no active roaming activation, Baobab Mobile will reverse the roaming charge in full. {grant}

## 7. International calls [international]

7.1 International calling is enabled on all contracts unless the customer asks to disable it. {other}

7.2 International calls are charged at the rate per minute in the tariff guide that was in force when the call was made. International calls made from the customer's line are payable by the customer, unless clause 8 applies. {limit}

## 8. Suspected fraud [fraud]

8.1 A customer who reports unauthorised use must report it as soon as they become aware of it. Baobab Mobile will investigate the report and will not decide liability until a fraud analyst has reviewed the records. {procedure}

8.2 While a fraud report is under investigation, the disputed amount is not payable and no credit is issued automatically. {condition}

## 9. Credits and limits [credits]

9.1 A credit never exceeds the amount charged less any credit already issued against the same charge. {limit}

9.2 Credits of R1,000.00 or less may be approved automatically. A larger credit needs approval from a billing specialist. {procedure}

9.3 A customer who is unhappy with the outcome of a query may escalate it to the Independent Communications Authority of South Africa. {procedure}
