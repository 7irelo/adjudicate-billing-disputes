# Karoo Connect Reseller Service Terms

Karoo Connect is a fictional mobile reseller created for a software demonstration. These terms do not describe any real business. Reference marker: KAROO-CANARY-3392.

## 1. Definitions [definitions]

1.1 "Billing month" means one calendar month. For proration, every billing month counts as 30 days. {definition}

1.2 "Dispute window" means the 30 calendar days after the date an invoice is issued. {definition}

1.3 "Effective cancellation date" means the last day of the notice period given under clause 5.1. {definition}

## 2. Billing and disputes [billing]

2.1 Karoo Connect issues one invoice for each billing month. All charges include VAT. {other}

2.2 A dispute must be raised within the dispute window. Karoo Connect gives no credit for a charge disputed after the dispute window, but a customer may ask for a manual review. {limit}

2.3 Where one charge for a service period is billed twice, Karoo Connect will credit the duplicate in full. {grant}

2.4 Where a usage charge is above the rate in force on the day of the call, Karoo Connect will credit the overcharge. {grant}

## 3. Out-of-bundle usage [out_of_bundle]

3.1 A customer agrees to out-of-bundle billing by choosing the "Keep Talking" option at sign-up. An out-of-bundle charge is valid only if that option was active for the whole service period of the charge. {condition}

3.2 Where out-of-bundle usage was charged without the "Keep Talking" option, Karoo Connect will reverse the charge in full. {grant}

## 4. Spend protection [spend_protection]

4.1 Karoo Connect has no bill-shock cap. Usage charges remain payable whatever their total in a billing month, and Karoo Connect gives no credit because usage was unusually high. {limit}

## 5. Cancellation [cancellation]

5.1 A customer may cancel by giving 20 business days' notice. The notice period starts on the business day after Karoo Connect receives the request. {condition}

5.2 A recurring charge for a billing month that begins on or before the effective cancellation date remains payable in full, with no proration for the remaining days of that month. {limit}

5.3 Karoo Connect will reverse in full a recurring charge for a billing month that begins after the effective cancellation date. {grant}

## 6. Roaming [roaming]

6.1 Roaming charges are payable by the customer once the customer has activated roaming. {limit}

6.2 Where roaming usage was charged with no roaming activation, Karoo Connect will reverse the charge in full. {grant}

## 7. International calls [international]

7.1 International calling must be enabled on request. International calls made while it is enabled are payable at the rate in force when the call was made, unless clause 8 applies. {limit}

## 8. Suspected fraud [fraud]

8.1 Karoo Connect refers every suspected case of fraud to its fraud desk. No credit is given until the fraud desk has reviewed the records. {procedure}

## 9. Credits and limits [credits]

9.1 A credit never exceeds the amount charged less any credit already issued against the same charge. {limit}

9.2 Credits of R500.00 or less may be approved automatically. A larger credit needs approval from an account manager. {procedure}

9.3 A customer who is unhappy with the outcome of a dispute may escalate it to the Independent Communications Authority of South Africa. {procedure}
