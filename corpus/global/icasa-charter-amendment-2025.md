# End-User and Subscriber Service Charter Amendment Regulations, 2025

Short extracts of regulation 8A, as substituted by Notice 7027 in Government Gazette No. 53991 of 23 January 2026. Clauses 8.1, 8.2 and 8.3 here correspond to 8A(1), 8A(2)(c) and 8A(3). The regulations come into force twelve months after publication, so this text does not govern disputes before 23 January 2027. Not legal advice. See corpus/manifest.yaml for the source.

## 8. Regulation 8A: voice, SMS and data services [regulation_8a]

8.1 A Licensee must send usage depletion notifications to end-users via SMS, push notification or any other applicable means, when usage reaches 50%, 80% and 100% depletion of each bundle. {procedure}

8.2 At all times, a Licensee must ensure that end-users are provided with the option to opt in to or opt out of out-of-bundle usage charges. {condition}

8.3 In the event that a service bundle is depleted, and the end-user has not opted in to out-of-bundle charges, a Licensee must not permit any out-of-bundle usage by, or out-of-bundle charges to, the end-user until such time that the end-user purchases a new bundle or opts into out-of-bundle usage and charges. {limit}
